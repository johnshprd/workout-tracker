# Workout Tracker — Setup Guide

Thank you for your purchase. Here's everything you need to get started in under two minutes.

---

## What you have

A single file: `workout-tracker.html`

That's your entire app. No servers, no databases, no monthly fees.

---

## Deploy in 60 seconds (recommended)

1. Go to **[netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop)**
2. Drag `workout-tracker.html` onto the page
3. Netlify gives you a live URL instantly — e.g. `https://sunny-croissant-abc123.netlify.app`
4. Open that URL on your phone, tap **Add to Home Screen** → it works like a native app

That's it. Free forever on Netlify's free tier.

## Your data

Everything is saved in your browser's **localStorage** — it stays on your device and persists between sessions. Clearing your browser data will erase your workout history, so treat the Netlify URL as permanent and don't clear site data for it.

---

## Customising the app

Open `workout-tracker.html` in any text editor (Notepad, VS Code, TextEdit).

### Change the app title
Search for `WORKOUT LOG` and replace with your own name.

### Change the colour scheme
The main accent colour is `#c8ff00` (lime green). Do a find-and-replace to swap it for any hex colour you like.

### Change default exercises
Find `DEFAULT_ROUTINE` in the script — edit the `name` fields to rename exercises, or reorder the list.

### Add your own exercises permanently
Use the **Routine** tab inside the app — no code needed.

## Personal Trainer use

Want to give clients their own version?

1. Open the file in a text editor
2. Change the title and colours to match your brand
3. Deploy a separate Netlify instance for each client (free)
4. Send them the link

Each client gets their own isolated app with their own data.
